❓ Frequently asked

  • How do the tilt controls work? The way you hold the phone when a run (or a new level) starts becomes “level”. Tilt left or right from that pose to steer the beam — it works both holding the phone in your hand and with it lying flat on a table.
  • The beam feels off-center. Pause and resume, or wait for the next level — the game re-calibrates “level” to however you hold the phone during the 3-2-1 countdown. There is also a sensitivity slider in Settings.
  • How do rounds work? A level is cleared by ten correct answers. A wrong answer sets your progress back by two and tips the world; running out of time tilts the world and shortens the beam.
  • What is Minute Rush? One 60-second round of non-stop questions: +1 for a correct answer, −1 for a wrong one. Your best score goes to its own leaderboard.
  • Do I need an account? No — you play as a guest with a generated name. Sign in with Google or Apple (Settings → Account) only if you want your record to survive reinstalls.
  • What does “Continue (watch an ad)” do? Once per run, after losing, you can watch a short rewarded ad to continue from where you died with your score and level intact. It is always optional.
  • How do I delete my data? Settings → Account → Delete account removes your leaderboard records and the account itself, permanently. See the Privacy Policy for details.
  • The game says I lost after three wrong answers. That's a rule, not a bug: three wrong answers in a row end the run — guessing by mashing buttons doesn't work in hell.

🐛 Reporting a bug

When something breaks, an email with your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing right before the problem helps us fix it much faster. Screenshots and screen recordings are gold.
